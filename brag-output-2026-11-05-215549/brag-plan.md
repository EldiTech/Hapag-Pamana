# HapagPamana launch video

## Planning rubric

- **Product:** HapagPamana Catering Services mobile app.
- **Audience:** People planning Filipino catering and memorable events.
- **Promise:** Bring menu discovery, event planning, booking, payment, and tracking into one thoughtful experience.
- **Hero feature:** The Book Us Now flow, supported by menu browsing and order tracking.
- **Proof:** Real project copy and screens: `BROWSE THE MENU`, `BOOK US NOW`, `Select Category`, `Allergen Summary`, `Tell us the occasion`, `Choose your venue`, `VIEW 3D LAYOUT`, `THE PAPER TRAIL`, and `Gabay — Your catering companion`.
- **Creative angle:** A celebration moves from first browse to fully held-together event.
- **Tone:** App-store polished, warm, premium, and trustworthy.
- **Format:** 1080 × 1920 portrait, 25 seconds.
- **Narration:** Warm English voiceover, 55 words, complementary to the visible UI.

## Storyboard

| Time | Scene | Visual | Narration |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–3s | Hook | Logo and `Lutong Pinoy, Pamanang Sarap`. | “From your first menu browse to the moments that matter,” |
| 3–7s | Reveal | App home with `BROWSE THE MENU` and `BOOK US NOW`. | “Hapag Pamana brings Filipino catering together in one thoughtful experience.” |
| 7–11s | Discover | Menu filters, `All Categories`, `Allergen Summary`, dietary preference. | “Explore dishes and catering packages,” |
| 11–16s | Book | Occasion, venue, add-ons, and booking progression. | “set your occasion, choose your venue, add the details that make it yours,” |
| 16–21s | Track | Order Tracking, 3D layout, menu, paper trail, downpayment. | “and keep every step in view.” |
| 21–25s | Companion / close | Gabay, `Your catering companion`, logo, `BOOK US NOW`. | “With Gabay by your side, your celebration starts simply—and ends memorably.” |

## Production notes

- Use the existing HapagPamana palette: cream, espresso, coffee brown, olive, and antique gold.
- Use the real logo and Gabay mark from `Assets/`.
- No invented dishes, prices, testimonials, statistics, or claims.
- Keep UI labels large enough for portrait social feeds.
