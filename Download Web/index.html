01 Origin

The table remembers

Small beginnings.
A generous future.

We tried to open a small canteen back in 2016. It did not work out — but it gave us a reason to keep showing up.

Hapag Pamana grew from that grit: a small family of people making ends meet, preserving the food and memories that made the work matter.
